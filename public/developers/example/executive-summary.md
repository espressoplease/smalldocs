# Project Meridian: executive summary

The analysis supports a staged entry into the Netherlands and Germany, starting with mid-market logistics teams. Demand is strongest where spreadsheet-based planning creates visible coordination costs.

> **Recommendation:** run a 90-day Netherlands pilot with three design partners before committing to the broader German launch.

## Decision at a glance

| Question | Finding | Implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where should we enter first? | Netherlands | Shorter sales cycle and concentrated buyer network |
| Who should we serve? | Logistics teams with 50 to 250 staff | Clear operational pain and reachable buying committee |
| How should we enter? | Design-partner pilot | Validate workflow fit before localising the full product |
| What is the main risk? | Integration effort | Prove two common data connections during the pilot |

## Why this sequence

~~~mermaid

  A[Three design partners] --> B[90-day pilot]
~~~

The plan holds the largest investment until product use, implementation time, and willingness to pay have been observed in the target market.

## Next actions

1. Recruit three Netherlands design partners from the existing logistics network.
2. Scope the two integrations needed by all three partners.
3. Agree pilot success measures before implementation begins.
4. Revisit the German launch decision after week eight.
