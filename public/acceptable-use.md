# Acceptable Use Policy

**Last updated: 24 August 2026**

This policy forms part of the [SmallDocs Terms of Service](/legal). It applies to the hosted website, encrypted short links, SmallDocs Cloud, and automated access to those services.

## Do not use the service to

- Break the law or help another person break the law.
- Infringe intellectual-property, privacy, confidentiality, or other rights.
- Store or distribute malware, phishing material, stolen credentials, or content intended to harm a device or person.
- Access an account, document, machine credential, system, or network without permission.
- Probe, bypass, or interfere with authentication, access controls, rate limits, billing controls, or security measures, except through a testing programme we have expressly authorised.
- Impersonate another person or misrepresent the source of content or a notification.
- Send spam, abusive invitations, or unwanted automated notifications.
- Place an unreasonable load on the hosted service or use automation in a way that materially affects other customers.
- Use the service to process content where doing so would require a legal, security, or compliance commitment that we have not agreed in writing.

## Automated use

Agents and scripts may use documented SmallDocs interfaces under an authorised human account. They must follow the same access rules as that person. Automation must not create accounts, invite people, broaden document access, or send notifications unless the authorised interface and account permissions allow it.

We do not charge per agent at launch. This does not grant unlimited compute or abusive traffic. If an automated workload creates a material operational cost, we will contact the account owner before imposing a specific limit or proposing a different plan, unless immediate action is needed to protect the service.

## Enforcement

We may remove hosted content or limit access when reasonably necessary to address a legal request, security risk, abuse, or breach of this policy. We may act immediately where delay would increase harm. In other cases, we will normally explain the concern and allow a reasonable opportunity to correct it.

Report abuse or ask whether a planned use is covered at [hi@smalldocs.org](mailto:hi@smalldocs.org).
