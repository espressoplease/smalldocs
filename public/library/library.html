How it works

  1. Choose your accountSubscribe for yourself or create a team account with members and permission groups.
  2. Add selected documentsOpen a document in SmallDocs and choose Add to Cloud. Your local file remains where it is.
  3. Find, organize, and shareSearch content and tags, choose who can open a document, and keep its revisions together.

Security and privacy

Cloud documents are encrypted at rest. SmallDocs checks each request, then decrypts documents the requester is allowed to access in application memory for viewing, search, and updates.

SmallDocs Cloud is not end-to-end encrypted. SmallDocs can access plaintext document content while serving an authorized request.
