## 1. What SmallDocs provides

Local SmallDocs reads and renders Markdown in your browser. Document content placed after the `#` in a SmallDocs URL is not sent to our server.

An encrypted short link uploads ciphertext to our server. Its decryption key remains in the URL fragment and is not sent to our server.

SmallDocs Cloud stores selected documents in personal and team libraries so authorised people and connected machines can open, search, edit, comment on, organise, export, and share them. SmallDocs manages the Cloud encryption keys and can decrypt an authorised document in application memory. Cloud is encrypted at rest, but it is not end-to-end encrypted or zero knowledge.

Publishing a Cloud document creates a separate public page that anyone can read without signing in and that search engines can index. Private comments, tags, source file paths, and revision history are excluded from the public copy. Unpublishing or deleting the source document removes public access through SmallDocs, though readers and search engines may retain copies.

The [Privacy Notice](/privacy) explains these data flows in more detail.
