SmallDocs Cloud

Evaluate Cloud

Use a Personal Cloud account for 30 days. This starts no paid subscription and asks for no payment method.

Cloud-first stays off until you enable it on a computer. After setup, running sdoc FILE.md creates or updates that file's Cloud copy before opening it. The CLI does not scan folders or watch files in the background.

At day 30, Cloud stops accepting changes. In this staging prototype, you can still read and export existing files. Retention after the review has not been decided. Use test documents only, and do not upload files you cannot risk losing during staging cleanup.
