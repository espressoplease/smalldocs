# Service Closure and Data Portability Policy

**Last updated: 24 August 2026**

This policy explains what Odd Solutions Ltd intends to do if SmallDocs Cloud closes permanently, is transferred to a successor, or the company ceases trading. It forms part of the [Terms of Service](/legal).

## A planned closure

If we decide to close SmallDocs Cloud and the process remains reasonably within our control, we will:

1. stop accepting new paid subscriptions and stop future renewals;
2. normally give at least 30 days' notice through the account email address and the service;
3. keep retained Cloud documents available in read-only form during that notice period;
4. keep authenticated document and workspace export available during that notice period; and
5. explain the final export deadline and what will happen to active data and backups after it.

A workspace export can include current documents, retained revisions, document metadata, projects, and account metadata in a documented machine-readable format. We will not send document content as an ordinary email attachment. The account owner must authenticate before requesting an export.

If we close a paid service before the end of a period we control and cannot provide access through the remainder of that period, we will refund the unused prepaid portion where we are legally and practically able to do so. An insolvency process may instead determine whether and how a payment claim is handled.

## After the export window

After the stated deadline, we will delete Cloud content from the active service unless law, an insolvency process, a security investigation, or a legal claim requires it to be retained. Encrypted copies may remain in backup archives until the published backup retention cycle completes. Billing and transaction records may be kept for the periods described in the [Privacy Notice](/privacy).

## Insolvency, legal restrictions, and sudden loss of service

A closure may occur with less notice or without an export window because of insolvency, a court or regulator's direction, loss of access to a provider, a security incident, or another event outside our reasonable control. If an administrator or liquidator is appointed, that practitioner may take control of decisions about the company and its data. Data-protection duties continue to apply during that process.

We cannot promise that Cloud or its export tools will remain available in every closure scenario. Keep an independent copy or periodic export of any document whose loss would cause serious harm.

## A transfer to a successor

A company reorganisation, sale, administration, or service transfer may involve a successor receiving account records or Cloud data. Any recipient must have a lawful basis for the transfer and must continue to protect personal data. Where reasonably practicable, we will give affected customers notice before a material transfer and provide an opportunity to export or delete retained Cloud content.

We will not treat a transfer as permission for a successor to use Cloud document content for unrelated advertising or marketing.
