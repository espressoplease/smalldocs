# Getting started with SmallDocs

Ask your coding agent to create a document. SmallDocs saves it in your Cloud library and gives you a link to open it.

## Create your first document

If you have not connected an agent, open [Install](/cloud/admin?panel=install) and paste the installation prompt into your agent's chat. Approve the connection in your browser when prompted.

Then try: "Create a document summarising our project, with decisions and next steps."

You can ask for a "document", "SmallDoc", or "sdoc". Once connected, your agent uses SmallDocs for document requests. Ask for a different format whenever you need one.

## Your library and tags

Your documents live in [Library](/library?scope=cloud). Open a document from any computer where you are signed in.

Your agent adds tags when it creates a document. Tags connect related work, such as `#customer-research` or `#release-planning`. Ask your agent to reuse tags from your library so documents stay easy to find.

Try: "Find our customer research documents and summarise the recurring themes."

The library selector lets you switch between your personal and team libraries. Your personal library is private. Create a team library when you want to add other people. Joining a team does not grant access to its private documents.

## Read, edit, and leave feedback

Use the icons above a document's title to switch between reading, commenting, and editing. They stay available while you scroll. In comment mode, select a passage or use a comment button beside a block to leave feedback.

Try: "Read the comments on this document, make the requested changes, and resolve the comments you address."

Your agent can make small edits, inspect revisions, and download the Markdown. Cloud edits keep the same document URL, and an open browser tab picks up saved changes. See [editing and comments](/cloud-updates) for commands and examples.

## Manage SmallDocs from chat

Ask your agent to create a team library, invite a member, change document access, search your library, or check your trial and billing status. It can open the relevant settings or payment page for you.

Try: "Invite Sam to our Engineering library", or "Open my billing settings so I can cancel my subscription."

You complete payment and cancellation in the billing page. Your agent can guide you and open that page.

## Explore document formats

A SmallDoc can combine text, charts, diagrams, code, spreadsheets, slides, and interactive examples. Browse [examples](/home#learn), or ask your agent to choose a format for the work you are doing.

## Share SmallDocs

Open Refer in the left menu to copy your referral link. People who join through it appear in your referral history. Once they connect their agent and make their first monthly payment, you earn credit for one person for one month.

Credits add up across the people you pay for. Five credits can cover five people for one month, or one person for five months. Unused credit carries forward. Ask your agent for your referral link or credit balance with `sdoc cloud referrals --json`.
