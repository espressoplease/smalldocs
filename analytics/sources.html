Visits by source

Each explicit ?src= value on the homepage or a short link creates a source here when its first visit is recorded. Every source gets its own weekly chart and table. Local #md document links are not source-attributed.

Create a source while sharing: sdoc share report.md --short --source email-launch

You can also add ?src=email-launch before the # in a short URL. Source names are stored in lowercase. X, YouTube, and LinkedIn aliases use their standard names.
